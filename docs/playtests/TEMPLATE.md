## Questions (ask right after playing, write down the exact words)
1. Did the jump feel good within the first 10 seconds? What would make it feel better?
2. Did crashes feel **fair**? Was any crash "not my fault"?
3. Did you want **one more go**? Why or why not?
4. When did it feel **coolest**? When did it feel boring?
5. Was anything confusing (controls, what to do, what killed you)?
6. Rockets: did you use them? Did you know when you could?
7. **Comfort and reach:** any hand or thumb strain? Is the hold too long or too short? Is the phone grip OK in landscape?
8. **Look and feel of the hero and chair:** does it feel badass and real? Anything that feels off or cringe?
9. What's one thing you'd change?
